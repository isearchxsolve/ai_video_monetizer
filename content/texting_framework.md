# THE TEXTING FRAMEWORK: 50 Copy-and-Paste High-Tension Scripts

*Premium bonus content for The Magnetism Blueprint customers*

---

## How to Use These Scripts

1. **Don't send them all at once** — Space them out. One per interaction max.
2. **Customize the bracketed parts** — Make them specific to your dynamic.
3. **Match their energy** — If they're playful, be playful. If they're serious, be mysterious.
4. **Never double-text** — Send one, wait for reply. Silence is your leverage.
5. **Track what works** — Note which scripts get the best responses for your style.

## PHASE 1: Opening Hooks (Days 1-3 of Contact)

**Purpose**: Break pattern, create curiosity, establish frame

1. `"You happened to cross my mind for a second just now. I blame a specific song."`
2. `"I just saw something that reminded me of you, but I'm keeping the details a secret for now."`
3. `"Are you always this distracting, or am I just catching you on an off day?"`
4. `"I have a highly specific question for you, and your answer will change how I look at you."`
5. `"Stop being so interesting. I actually have work to finish today."`
6. `"You have a dangerous amount of chaotic energy, you know that?"`
7. `"I just had a highly entertaining thought about our last conversation."`
8. `"My phone just lit up, and for a split second, I actually hoped it was you."`
9. `"You owe me a coffee. I'm not telling you why yet, just write it down."`
10. `"I was just thinking about how much trouble you probably cause on a daily basis."`

## ADVANCED TACTICS

### The "Triangle Glance" Text Sequence
Send these three in sequence over 24-48 hours:
1. `"Left eye."` (wait for confusion/reply)
2. `"Lips."` (wait)
3. `"Right eye. You're welcome."`

### The "Cliffhanger" Exit
When conversation is peaking:
> `"Anyway, I have to go do [cool thing]. Don't get into too much trouble without me."`

Then **ghost for 4-6 hours minimum**.

### The "Callback" Re-open
Reference something from days ago:
> `"Still thinking about what you said about [topic]. You have a weirdly specific brain."`

### The "Disqualification" Pivot
When they compliment you:
> `"Careful. Flattery gets you everywhere. But arrogance gets you blocked. Which are you?"`

## RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

| DO | DON'T |
|----|-------|
| Match their text length (or go shorter) | Send paragraphs when they send sentences |
| Use declarative statements | Ask permission ("Can I...", "Is it okay if...") |
| Leave threads open | Resolve every conversation |
| Vary response time (5 min → 4 hrs → 20 min) | Reply instantly every time |
| Use callbacks to previous threads | Treat each text as isolated |
| End on high tension | End on "ok" "cool" "lol" |
